03 Talent Acquisition & Onboarding Lifecycle

The recruitment module integrates applicant tracking directly with employee provisioning. Candidates advance through structured stages until hiring automatically initiates the cross-department onboarding engine.

Step 1: Requisition & Sourcing

HR creates job postings specifying department, vacancies, closing dates, and hiring manager. Candidates register with contact information, experience, and expectations.

Step 2: Technical & Managerial Interviews

Interviews are scheduled with calendar times and secure meeting URLs. Assigned interviewers submit quantitative scores (1 to 5) and qualitative evaluation notes directly on the platform.

Step 3: One-Click Hiring & Employee Auto-Provisioning

When the candidate is moved to HIRED, the system triggers a transactional creation workflow: generating an employee code (EMP-100X), linking reporting managers, initializing leave balances, creating salary structures, and generating initial login credentials.

Step 4: Cross-Functional Onboarding Checklist

The system auto-generates department-specific onboarding items across HR (document verification), IT (hardware provisioning & credentials), Manager (1-on-1 alignment), and Employee (compliance training).

Live Attendance Punch Clock

Employees punch in upon arrival. The system computes standard work hours (8h), flags late arrivals after 09:30 AM, logs overtime, and allows punch-out upon day completion.

Leave Quota Cards

Interactive cards display total, consumed, and remaining allowances for Annual, Casual, and Sick leaves for the current calendar year with instant application submission.

Attendance Correction Requests

If an employee misses a punch or encounters transit delays, they submit an attendance regularization note with requested times for manager review.

Confidential Payslip Access

Employees securely review and download their monthly compensation breakdowns, showing allowances, statutory deductions, overtime bonuses, and net take-home pay.

05 Attendance Engine & Leave Governance

The platform enforces rigorous operational rules to ensure fair timekeeping and automated policy compliance.

Standard Working Hours & Late Threshold

Standard workday is calculated at 8.0 hours. Any check-in registered past 09:30 AM automatically logs late minutes and tags the record status as LATE for supervisor visibility.

Overtime Calculation Formula

Time recorded in excess of the standard 8.0 hours is tracked as overtime hours. During monthly compensation processing, approved overtime is dynamically calculated and added to the employee's payroll allowances.

Leave Approval & Overlap Protection Workflow

The leave engine automatically excludes Saturdays and Sundays from leave calculations, prevents overlapping date bookings, and routes requests to the reporting manager with instant in-app alerts.

07 In-App Real-Time Notification & Popup Toast System

To ensure critical HR events are never overlooked, the platform incorporates a floating notification toast system across the entire application shell.

Automated Notification Triggering

Notifications are automatically dispatched when leave requests are submitted, interviews are scheduled, candidates accept offers, attendance corrections are requested, or compensation runs are completed.

Interactive Floating Popup Card

When an authenticated user (Admin, HR, Manager, or Employee) opens their dashboard or portal, unread notifications appear as animated floating cards on the lower-right corner of the workspace.

Instant Dismissal & "Mark as Read"

Each popup card provides an Open link and a Mark as Read button. Clicking "Mark as Read" makes an asynchronous background call to the database, decrements the topbar bell badge, and animates the card off the screen.
